WORD SEARCH GAME

Welcome to the Word Search Game, where your goal is to find hidden words within a grid of letters. As you begin the game, you are presented with a square grid filled with random letters. These words are cleverly hidden in different directions. They may appear horizontally, vertically, or diagonally, and can even be spelled backward. It is up to you to carefully scan the grid and spot each word!

As you explore the word search puzzle, you try to find words hidden within the grid of letters. When you think you have spotted a word, you type it into the game. The system will then check your answer. If the word you entered matches one of the hidden words in the puzzle, the game will confirm that the word is correct and mark it as found. However, if the word is not part of the puzzle, the game will display a message saying that the word does not exist.

To keep things even more exciting, the game has three types of categories: Fruits, Car Brands, and Countries. Each level is unique, each with its own theme and carefully hidden words that will really test your skills. But here is the best part, if you conquer all three levels, you win a special reward: a Jaypee Peroy!
